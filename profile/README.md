**bristlecone lab** is an independent research group that studies the Earth and the cosmos from open data.
Each project is an atlas you can open in a web browser, with its code and data published alongside it.

### Atlases

| Atlas | What it shows | Built on | Code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Global Seismicity Atlas](https://seismicityatlas.org) | Where earthquake catalogues are complete, and the b-value with its uncertainty | ISC-GEM, USGS ComCat | [repo](https://github.com/ismoilovdevml/seismicity-atlas) |
| [Volcano Blind Spots](https://volcano.bristleconelab.org) | Where open GPS and radar data would miss magma building up beneath a volcano | NGL GNSS, Sentinel-1 | [repo](https://github.com/bristleconelab/volcano-detectability) |
| [Seafloor Atlas](https://seaflooratlas.org) | The ocean floor in 3D, and the 30.8% of it that ships have measured | GEBCO 2026 | [repo](https://github.com/ismoilovdevml/seafloor-atlas) |
| [Ocean Life Atlas](https://oceanlife.bristleconelab.org) | The animals and plants recorded in any part of the sea | OBIS, WoRMS, Wikidata | [repo](https://github.com/bristleconelab/ocean-life-atlas) |
| [Lake Atlas](https://lakes.bristleconelab.org) | 1.4 million lakes, coloured by the evidence for their depth: published, seen from space, or model only | HydroLAKES, 3D-LAKES, Wikidata | [repo](https://github.com/bristleconelab/lakes-atlas) |
| [Underworld](https://underworld.bristleconelab.org) | The ocean floors that sank into the mantle, the plates diving under trenches and the earthquakes inside them, in 3D | Atlas of the Underworld, USGS Slab2, USGS ComCat | [repo](https://github.com/bristleconelab/underworld-3d) |
| [Cosmic Atlas](https://cosmic.bristleconelab.org) | 39.6 million stars and 15 million galaxies, each at its measured position | Gaia, DESI | [repo](https://github.com/bristleconelab/cosmic-atlas) |

### Research code

| Project | What it does | Built on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [glof-watch](https://github.com/bristleconelab/glof-watch) | Measures glacial lakes in every clear satellite scene and checks a watchlist each week for fast growth or sudden drainage | Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, Glacial Lake Observatory |
| [fossil-scout](https://github.com/bristleconelab/fossil-scout) | Ranks ground by how much it looks like known Hell Creek fossil sites, tested on an area the model never saw | Sentinel-2, Copernicus DEM, Paleobiology Database |
| [iceland-volcano-watch](https://github.com/bristleconelab/iceland-volcano-watch) | How much magma has collected beneath Svartsengi, and how sure we can be | NGL GNSS, Sentinel-1, IMO earthquakes |
| [vesuvius-pipeline](https://github.com/bristleconelab/vesuvius-pipeline) | A reproducible, CPU-only pipeline for reading ink in the Herculaneum scrolls | Vesuvius Challenge scans |
